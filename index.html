The Formula: K = i × c where i = invitations sent per customer · c = conversion rate of those invitations

Where K-Factor Lives in the Loop

K-factor is the bridge across the loop. Every customer who advocates (left side) and triggers a new acquisition (top right) is one unit of K. Strengthening that arc — better refer-a-friend mechanics, more shareable unboxing, deeper post-purchase bond — directly raises K and tightens the cycle time between Advocate and Acquisition.

The two halves:

  • CAQ (top) — Evaluate · Consider · Customer Acquisition · Purchase. Paid media plus referrals do the work here.
  • LTV (bottom) — Experience · Bond · Advocate. Product, NPS, and community decide how high c can get.

How To Read This Model

K-factor measures the new customers each existing customer generates via referrals/word-of-mouth. K above 1.0 produces self-sustaining exponential growth; below 1.0 still meaningfully offsets paid CAC.

  • Refer-a-Friend typically delivers the highest K because the incentive directly drives both invitation volume and conversion.
  • Social Unboxing / UGC has lower conversion per impression but enormous reach — small c on a large effective i still moves the blended K.
  • Organic Word-of-Mouth is the cheapest channel ($0 incentive cost) but is gated by product love — improve NPS and the c climbs.
  • Cycle time matters as much as K. A K of 0.4 with a 30-day cycle outperforms a K of 0.6 with a 90-day cycle over a year.

Effective Referral CAC = (refer-a-friend reward × successful referrals) ÷ new customers from all referral channels. Compare this to paid CAC to size the offset.
